Privacy Policy

What DanceMax collects, what stays on your phone, and the choices you have.

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version

Your camera is never uploaded, and your dance videos stay on your phone unless you choose to start a battle on one. Then that one video is sent to our storage so the people you invite can dance to it, and it is taken down after the battle. If you make an account, we store your email, the answers you gave when you set up, and your scores. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

Videos you share in a battle

When you start a battle on one of your videos, a copy of that video and the dancer's skeleton data worked out from it are uploaded to private storage. Only the people who join that battle with your link or code can download them, and they get the same files so everyone dances to the same video. The copy on our storage can't be downloaded by anyone two hours after the battle was opened, and it is deleted when you remove the battle or the next time you open DanceMax after the battle. Copies on the other players' phones are deleted a day after the battle. Videos received this way are not added to the other players' own videos. Nothing is uploaded unless you start a battle on it. Anyone in a battle can report its video to us.

Videos you send in to be featured

From Home you can choose to send us a video of you dancing: one of your replay recordings, or a video from your Photos. You are asked to agree first, and you can send one a week. A copy is uploaded to our storage with your first name, the dance's name and your score. Only you and we can see it, unless we pick it: a video we pick is shown on the Home page of the app to everyone who uses DanceMax, with your first name, and may be used when we promote DanceMax. You can take a video back at any time from the same screen; it is then deleted from our storage and comes off Home. Deleting your account deletes the videos you sent in.

What stays on your phone

Camera: DanceMax uses the front camera to follow your body while you dance. Frames are processed on the device and are never uploaded.

Videos: videos you import and the pose data worked out from them are stored in DanceMax's private folder on your phone, and are sent nowhere unless you start a battle on one. The replay recordings of your runs are stored only on your phone, and are sent nowhere unless you choose to send one in to be featured. Deleting a dance, or the app, removes them.

Automatic check: every video you import, send to a battle, send in to be featured or receive in a battle is checked on your phone for nudity and sexual content, and can't be used if it is flagged. The check runs on the device; the video isn't sent anywhere for it.

Profile photo: if you set one, it is stored only on your phone.

Photos: DanceMax sees only the videos you pick.

What an account stores

When you sign in (with Apple, Google or an email and password) we store: your email address; the name you entered; your setup answers (why you're here, your level, the styles you chose, what gets in your way, how often you want to practice); and your scores (which dance, the score, the number of Perfect, Good and Miss beats, and when).

If you start or join a battle, the name you entered and your score in it, updated as you dance, are stored with the battle and shown to the other people in it, along with the video the host picked (see the section on videos you share in a battle). Removing the battle from your list removes them. Battles need an account.

We do not store video, camera frames or skeleton data.

Why we use it

To sign you in, to keep your plan and scores safe, and to make the app work. That's all.

Who handles it

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States.

Signing in: if you sign in with Apple or Google, that company confirms who you are and gives us your email address and, if you allow it, your name. We don't receive your password.

Subscriptions: purchases are made through Apple, and we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to keep track of whether a subscription is active; it receives your DanceMax account's ID number and your purchase history for DanceMax (which plan, when, and whether it is in a free trial), not your name, email or anything you do in the app.

We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't use advertising or analytics services.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting it in Profile removes your account, your answers and your scores from our servers for good.

Your choices

You can change your answers in Profile, sign out, or delete your account at any time. You can turn off camera access in Settings; scoring won't work without it. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data or to have it corrected or deleted.

Children

DanceMax is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can read and change only its own data, apart from the name, score and battle video you share with the other people in a battle. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to the minimum.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, this policy will be updated and the date below will change. Significant changes will be shown in the app.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent through the support contact on DanceMax's App Store page.

You can also reach us from the Support page.
