How it works

Three steps. About a minute to your first score.

  1. 1

    Pick a dance

    Start with a built-in dance, or upload any dance video from your Photos. DanceMax works out the moves on your phone.

  2. 2

    Dance along

    Prop up your iPhone and step back. The front camera follows your body and compares it to the dancer in real time.

  3. 3

    See your score

    Every beat is rated Perfect, Good or Miss, and it all adds up to a score out of 100. Watch the replay, then go again.

Everything you need to get a dance down.

Questions

Good to know.

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and enough room to step back so the front camera can see you.

Which videos can I use?

Any dance video in your Photos that you made or have permission to use. DanceMax can't download videos from the internet.

Is my camera recorded or uploaded?

No. Camera frames are processed on your phone and never uploaded. Replay recordings of your runs are stored only on your phone unless you choose to send one in to be featured.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple Account settings: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. If you cancel during the free trial you are not charged.

How accurate are the scores?

Scores and ratings are estimates made by software from your camera. They are for practice and fun, not a professional assessment.
