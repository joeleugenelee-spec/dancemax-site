Terms of Use

The rules for using DanceMax, including how the subscription and free trial work.

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version

DanceMax helps you learn dances by scoring how closely you match a video. You need a subscription to use it. Dance safely, only import videos you have the right to use, and don't misuse the app.

Who can use DanceMax

You must be at least 13. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms for you. You need an account to use the app, and you are responsible for keeping your sign-in details to yourself.

Subscription and free trial

DanceMax is a paid app. There are two plans: DanceMax Plus Yearly and DanceMax Plus Monthly. The price of each is shown in the app before you buy. The yearly plan may start with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial if you have one. Your subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. If you cancel during a free trial, you are not charged and access ends when the trial does.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions). Deleting the app or your DanceMax account does not cancel a subscription. Purchases are handled by Apple, and refunds are requested from Apple.

Videos you import

You can import videos from your Photos library. Only import videos you made or have permission to use. You keep all rights to your videos; they stay on your phone and are not uploaded to us. DanceMax cannot download videos from the internet.

If you believe something in DanceMax infringes your rights, use "Report content or request a takedown" in Profile.

Dance safely

Dancing is physical activity. Make sure you have clear space around you, stop if you feel pain or dizziness, and talk to a doctor first if you have a health condition that exercise could affect. You use DanceMax at your own risk.

Scores and feedback

Scores, ratings and tips are estimates made by software from your camera. They can be wrong, and they are for practice and fun, not a professional assessment.

What you may not do

Don't copy, resell or reverse engineer the app, try to get around the subscription, use it to break the law or someone else's rights, or interfere with how it works for others.

Videos you share in battles

When you start a battle on one of your videos, you send that video to the people you invite. Only share videos you made or have the right to share, and nothing that is illegal, sexual, hateful or that shows someone who hasn't agreed to it. You stay responsible for what you share. You give DanceMax permission to store the video and deliver it to the people in that battle, for that purpose only. Anyone in a battle can report its video; we may remove any video and suspend accounts that share videos they shouldn't, including accounts that repeatedly share videos that infringe someone's copyright. If you believe a video shared through DanceMax infringes your copyright, contact us and we will remove it.

Videos you send in to be featured

If you send in a video to be featured, you confirm that you are the person in it or have that person's permission, and that you have the right to share it. You give DanceMax permission to store it, to show it on the Home page of the app to all users, and to use it when promoting DanceMax, until you take it back. We choose which videos are shown, and may decline or remove any video.
