Support

How can we help?

Answers to the most common questions are below. If you're still stuck, email us and we'll get back to you.

Email support@dancemax.org

Subscription and billing

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose DanceMax. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app or your DanceMax account does not cancel a subscription.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan may start with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. If you cancel during the trial you are not charged, and access ends when the trial does.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Camera and scoring

The app isn't scoring me

Check that DanceMax has camera access in Settings, then prop up your phone and step back until the front camera can see you clearly. Good, even lighting helps.

A score looks wrong

Scores and ratings are estimates made by software from your camera, and they can be wrong. Try again with more space and light. If it keeps happening on the same dance, email us and tell us which one.

Your videos

How do I add my own dance video?

Import it from your Photos library inside the app. Only import videos you made or have permission to use. DanceMax can't download videos from the internet.

Why can't I use a video?

Every video is checked on your phone for nudity and sexual content, and can't be used if it is flagged. The check runs on the device; the video isn't sent anywhere for it.

Battles

How do I battle a friend?

Start a battle on one of your videos and send the invite link or the six-character code. Battles need an account, and everyone you invite dances to the same video at the same moment.

What happens to the video I share?

It is sent to DanceMax so the people you invite can dance to it. Only they can get it, for two hours, and it is deleted after the battle.

Account and content

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete account. This removes your account, your answers and your scores from our servers for good. Cancel your subscription separately in your Apple Account settings.

How do I report a video or request a takedown?

Use "Report content or request a takedown" in Profile, or email us with the details and we will look at it.
